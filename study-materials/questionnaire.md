**Project Title:** Expert Evaluation of FORAP (Framework for Organizing Reusable and Adaptable PjBL Projects) for Computing Project-Based Learning.

**Researcher(s):** [Omitted for anonymous review]

**Institutional Review Board (IRB) approval number:** [Omitted for anonymous review]

You have been asked to be a part of the research described here. Participation is voluntary.

**Summary of the study:** This study is for computing instructors and other individuals with relevant experience in computing project-based learning (PjBL). The purpose of the study is to evaluate FORAP, a framework for organizing reusable and adaptable PjBL projects in computing education. Participants will be asked to review FORAP and then provide structured feedback on the framework through an online questionnaire. The goal is to better understand the strengths of the framework and identify areas for improvement.

There are no known risks to you for completing the questionnaire.

Please continue to read the material below if you are interested in participating in this study.

**What to expect:** You will be asked to review a brief set of FORAP materials before completing an online questionnaire about the framework. These materials include a short video presentation and a reference website. The questionnaire will include rating-scale items and open-ended questions. The study should take about 15-20 minutes to complete, including the time needed to review the materials. The questionnaire is completely anonymous.
